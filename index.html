THE BIG PICTURE

Why waste awareness
matters to all of us.

When waste is mixed or dumped carelessly, it can pollute land and water, attract pests, and make public spaces less healthy. Better everyday habits can help prevent these problems.

🌿

Protect nature

Keep litter out of streets, drains, parks, and waterways to help protect local ecosystems.

01 / ENVIRONMENT
🏘️

Healthier neighbourhoods

Cleaner surroundings support community wellbeing and make shared spaces more welcoming.

02 / COMMUNITY
🔄

Save useful resources

Reusing and recycling suitable materials can reduce demand for new raw resources.

03 / RESOURCES
SORT IT RIGHT

Every waste type
has a right place.

Separate waste at the source. Follow your local municipal collection rules, because accepted materials and bin colours can vary by area.

🍌WET
BIODEGRADABLE

Wet / organic waste

Kitchen scraps and garden waste that can often be composted.

ExamplesFruit peels · Vegetable scraps · Tea leaves · Dry leaves
✓ Keep it separate for composting where available.
📦DRY
REUSABLE / RECYCLABLE

Dry waste

Clean, dry materials that may be reused or recycled through the right collection stream.

ExamplesPaper · Cardboard · Metal cans · Clean containers
✓ Keep recyclable items clean and dry.
🔋E-WASTE
SPECIAL HANDLING

Electronic waste

Old electronics and batteries need appropriate collection, not ordinary bins.

ExamplesPhones · Cables · Batteries · Small devices
✓ Use an authorised e-waste collection point.
⚠️SPECIAL
HANDLE CAREFULLY

Hazardous / sanitary waste

Some household products and sanitary waste require safe, separate handling.

ExamplesPaint or chemical containers · Medicines · Sanitary waste
✓ Follow municipal guidance; never mix chemicals.

Note: These are general awareness guidelines, not a substitute for your local municipality's waste collection instructions.
